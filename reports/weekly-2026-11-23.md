1522 posts from 400 resorts in the last 7 days (514 accounts checked). Outperformed = at least 3x that account's own normal.

THIS WEEK
Mid-November social strategy shifted heavily toward early-season storm proof and operational opening prep. While reels comprised 35 percent of all 1,522 tracked posts across 400 resorts, they over-indexed among top performers at 43 percent, driven by raw video of live snowfall and maintenance work. Mammoth Mountain generated 2,641,735 views on a reel (14.8 times its normal baseline), its best reel in 24 days, by sharing early morning storm footage. Behind-the-scenes infrastructure updates also outperformed; small resort Big Rock Mountain punched above its 1,955 followers with a reel logging 147,147 views (45.9 times normal), its best reel in eight months, by showing lift tower removal.

Visual proof of opening readiness drove strong reach across mid and major resorts. Ober Mountain earned 318,867 views on a reel (37.3 times normal) showing snowcats preparing the hill for opening. Snowbasin secured six top slots, highlighted by a feed post sharing a major resort award that pulled 4,384 likes (16.4 times normal), its best post in 37 days.
