Ski resort Instagram: week ending Nov 16, 2026

1469 posts from 388 resorts in the last 7 days (514 accounts checked). Outperformed = at least 3x that account's own normal.

THIS WEEK
Mid-November performance was driven by first snowfall announcements and early snowmaking updates as resorts prepare for opening day. Short-form video overperformed relative to feed posts, representing 43% of the top 21 posts despite accounting for only 32% of the 1,469 total posts tracked across 388 resorts this week. Mid-sized accounts generated massive baseline multipliers by posting real-time snow footage. Ober Mountain achieved mega-viral reach with a reel delivering 3,032,574 views—475.4 times its normal 6,379 median—marking its best reel performance in eight months. Sunrise Park Resort generated 1,965 likes on a feed post, exceeding its 45-like median by 43.7 times.

Photo posts documenting fresh snow also saw high engagement multiples. Snowshoe Mountain earned 13,057 likes (42.3 times its 309 median), recording its top post in two months. Behind-the-scenes operational content allowed smaller resorts to punch above their weight. Small resort Holiday Mountain logged 121,655 views (11.0 times its 11,000 median) sharing a chairlift load test, while Bristol Mountain secured 2,609 likes (26.6 times its median of 98), its best post in 11 months.
